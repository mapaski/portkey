The problem

Shared computers remember more than you think.

College labs, airports, hotels, cyber cafés and co-working spaces can expose passwords, user profiles, files and session data to the next person who sits down.

🔑

Credentials at risk

Saved passwords and open sessions can be exposed if a shared device is infected or monitored.

📁

Files left behind

Downloads, cache and browsing history stay on the machine long after you leave.

💼

Today's fixes fall short

Laptops are heavy to carry, and VPNs do not protect against keyloggers or compromised systems.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is Portkey available now?

Not yet. We are building a working version of the extension and opening early access to a small group first. Join the waitlist to be among the first.

Who is Portkey for?

Students using college labs, travelers, freelancers and remote professionals who sometimes have to work on a shared or public computer. Libraries, cyber cafés, co-working spaces and IT teams can contact us about team plans.

Where are my files stored?

In encrypted cloud storage (AES-256), not on the shared computer. When the session ends, local session data is cleared.

Is it safe on a PC that already has malware?

No tool can promise perfect safety on an infected machine. Portkey reduces the data left behind and limits exposure, and we recommend avoiding sensitive work on devices you do not trust.

Which browsers will it support?

We are starting with Chromium-based browsers like Chrome and Edge, and will expand based on demand.
